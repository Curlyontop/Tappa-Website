Tap. Review. Done.

Tappa provides NFC Google review cards for businesses. Customers can tap their compatible phone on a Tappa card to access the business's Google review page.

Important Tappa Information

Tappa is a New Zealand business.

Tappa provides NFC cards that can take customers to a business's Google review page.

The customer taps their compatible phone on the Tappa card and is taken to the business's Google review page.

Tappa is designed to make it easier for businesses to give customers a simple way to access their Google review page.
