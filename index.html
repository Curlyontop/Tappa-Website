What is Tappa?

Tappa provides NFC Google review cards for businesses. The cards give customers a simple way to access a business's Google review page by tapping their compatible phone on the card.

Tappa is designed to make it easier for businesses to ask customers for Google reviews.

Why use Tappa?

  • Makes accessing a Google review page simple.
  • Uses NFC technology for a quick tap experience.
  • Useful for businesses that want to make asking for Google reviews easier.
  • Can be placed in a convenient location where customers can see it.
